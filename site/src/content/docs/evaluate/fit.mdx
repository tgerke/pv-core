The verdicts below come straight from the readiness table on the
[roadmap](/pv-core/roadmap/#what-a-build-us-a-safety-database-engagement-needs), read at
build time, so this page can never say something the roadmap does not. Two ground rules
carry over from there: a gap is not a commitment, and a boundary is not a gap.

Tick the needs your engagement has. The summary fills in as you go and stays in your
browser; when you are done, copy it, add your notes, and send it to whoever pointed you
here.

## Also worth knowing

Facts that come up in the first conversation and are documented elsewhere on this site:

- **Blinded studies.** The randomization system breaks the blind; pv-core records the fact
  (arm, when, why, code-break reference), and the arm appears only where the record needs
  it ([clocks and submissions](/pv-core/user-guide/clocks-and-submissions/#waivers-unblinding-nullification)).
- **Several sponsors on one instance.** Sponsors are a scope, not a tenant: segregation
  rests on grants and scoped reads, and there are no per-sponsor keys or isolation
  guarantees ([compliance, honest gaps](/pv-core/compliance/#honest-gaps-current-phase)).
- **The EDC.** A source system can push cases in through an intake identity that creates
  cases and attachments and reads nothing back ([intake](/pv-core/user-guide/intake-and-data-entry/#intake-from-the-edc));
  reconciliation of the EDC's serious events against pv-core's cases is not built yet
  ([roadmap](/pv-core/roadmap/#edc-reconciliation)).
- **Sign-on and signatures.** A pilot runs against your identity provider with OIDC, and
  signing requires re-authentication; the demo's dev tokens are not a Part 11 posture
  ([compliance](/pv-core/compliance/)).
- **Your data team.** The web app consumes only the public API, and every derived view is
  readable over a read-only SQL connection or from R ([SQL access](/pv-core/sql-access/),
  [cookbook](/pv-core/cookbook/)).
- **Hosting.** One instance per CRO, run by the deploying organization from published
  container images; there is no hosted service ([getting started](/pv-core/getting-started/)).
- **MedDRA.** Coding is against a licensed release you load yourself; nothing licensed
  ships in the repository, and the demo dictionary is a labeled illustrative subset
  ([getting started](/pv-core/getting-started/#meddra)).
- **Which regulators.** The seeded reporting rules cover FDA IND safety reports, EU SUSARs,
  and investigator and IRB notifications. Destinations and rules are data an
  administrator adds, so another authority is configuration against your own verified
  texts, not a code change ([administration](/pv-core/user-guide/administration/#destinations-and-reporting-rules)).
- **Validation.** It is not validated software. The raw material for a validation program
  (IQ and OQ reports, a requirement-to-test traceability matrix) is generated from live
  runs; the program itself is organizational work ([validation](/pv-core/validation/)).

## Send it back

Copy the summary above, add what the checkboxes cannot say (the workflow it concerns, who
does it and how often, the regulator or destination involved, how you handle it today, and
what done would look like), and send it to the person who gave you this link. That is how
the roadmap gets its next arc.

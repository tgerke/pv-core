This section is for the person deciding whether pv-core could run a sponsor's safety
database: a head of clinical operations or pharmacovigilance at a CRO, a sponsor's safety
lead, or the QA person they bring along. It shows the product as it is today, screen by
screen, and is honest about what is not there yet.

pv-core is a pharmacovigilance case-management system for the sponsor and CRO seat,
clinical-trial first. A CRO runs one instance and hosts several sponsors on it, with access
scoped to a sponsor organization or to a single study. Every case is an ICH E2B(R3)-shaped
record with versions that lock on signature; reporting obligations are materialized from
rules and their status is derived on every read; and 21 CFR Part 11 is a property of the
schema, not a feature toggle.

## Things to ask for on a screen share

The demo rewards specific requests. These are the ones that show the most:

- Open a case whose clock is overdue and show why each rule applies to it.
- Switch to the auditor persona and try to change anything.
- Open the placebo subject and show the waived obligations and the unblinding fact.
- Render the CIOMS I form from the approved case and point at the version hash in the footer.
- Verify the audit chain, then open one audit row and show what changed.
- Open the same queue as the other sponsor's case processor.

## What it deliberately does not do

Some things incumbents sell are things pv-core has decided not to do: subject-level
adverse-event capture (the EDC owns it), breaking the blind (the randomization system does;
pv-core records the fact), gateway transmission to regulators (pv-core produces the files
and records what was sent), the post-marketing workflow, signal detection, and the
validation program itself. They are restated on the [roadmap](/pv-core/roadmap/#deliberate-boundaries-not-gaps)
so that a feature comparison stays clean, and the [fit assessment](/pv-core/evaluate/fit/)
labels them as boundaries rather than gaps.
